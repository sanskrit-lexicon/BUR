# Sanskrit Roots, 7

[printed page number: 773]

## Left column

स्तेन् *stén*

गुध् **gudh* *guḍ* *guṇḍ* *kuṇḍ* *kuḍ* *skuḍ* *spuḍ* *ćuḍ* *ćuḍ* *ćauḍ* *ćand* *luḍ* *bhruḍ* *muñṭ* to cover, to hide, to protect. Cf. *guh*.

गृध् **gṛdh* *gardh* *gâdh* *nâdh* to desire.

बन्ध् **bandh* *bundh* *ant* to bind.

बाध् **bâdh* *vâdh* *badh* *vadh* to strike, to torment.

मृध् **mṛdh** to be moist.

युध् **yudh** to fight.

रध् **radh* *ruṭ* *luṭ* *uṭ* *ût* to stretch on the ground, to kill.

राध् **râdh* *sâdh* *sidh* to complete, to perfect.

रुध् **rudh** to obstruct.

शुध् **çudh* *çundh** to be cleaned.

स्पर्ध् **spardh** to rival.

ऊन् **ûn** to diminish.

कन् **kan** to shine, to see; to love (cf. *kam*).

खन् **khan** to dig.

जन् **jan** to engender.

तन् **tan** to stretch.

तन् **tan* *van* to have faith.

दान् **dán** to be ou to make straight.

धन् **dhan** to bear fruit.

पन् **pan** to praise, to approve.

मन् **man** penser.

वन् **van* *ban* *san* to worship.

शान् **çân* (*çô*) to sharpen.

## Right column

शठ् *çaṭh*

स्वन **swan*, *kwaṇ* *kuṇ* *kaṇ* *aṇ*, *ćan*, *dhwan* *dhan* *tan* *stan*, *dhwaṇ* *dhaṇ* *dhwaraṇ* *dhraṇ*, *van* *van*, *vaṇ* *baṇ* *maṇ* *bhraṇ* *bhraṇ* *vraṇ* *raṇ* to resound (to cry).

हन् **han** to push, to kill.

कीट् **kîṭ* *puṭ* to bind.

कुट्ट् **kuṭṭ* *kûṭ* to be hot.

क्षोट् **xóṭ* *kóṭ* *kóḍ* to hurl; || *ćiṭ* to send.

घट् **ghaṭ** to strive. || *guṭ* *ćuṭ* *ruṭ* *ruṭ* *luṭ* *luṇṭ* *luṭ* résister, to retort.

घट्ट् **ghaṭṭ** to shake.

चुट् **ćuṭ* *ćuṭṭ* *ćuṇṭ* *ćuṇḍ* *suṭṭ* *puṭ* *puṭṭ* to be small; || *laṭ* to be a child.

चेष्ट् **céṣṭ** to roll about.

जट् **jaṭ* *jhaṭ* to be twisted.

तट् **taṭ** to be high.

पिट् **piṭ* *viṭ** to resound; || *viṭ* *viḍ* *biṭ* *hiṭ*, *raṭ* *réṭ* *laṭ*, to cry (to speak), *tuṭ* to quarrel.

यौट् **yæṭ* *yæḍ* to stick (cf. *yu*).

रुण्ट् **ruṇṭ* *runṭ* *ruṇḍ* *luṇṭ* *lunṭ* *luṇḍ* to steal.

वट् **vaṭ* *vaṇṭ* *vaṇḍ* to share.

वट् **vaṭ* *vaṇḍ* *maṇḍ* *véṣṭ* to clothe (to adorn). Cf. *vas*.

सट् **saṭ** to form part of.

सट्ट् **saṭṭ** to be strong (to take, to kill, to dwell, to give).

हट् **haṭ** to shine.

कण्ठ् **kaṇṭh** to regret, cf. *kâyx*.
