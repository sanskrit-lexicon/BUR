# Sanskrit Suffixes, 3

[printed page number: 763]

## Left column

उस् *us*

**ima** (*i*, *ma*; ou *ma* avec *i* de liaison) adj. and n. noun *lyagima* (*lyaj*) abandoned, *talima* n. ground prepared for a building. Cf. *ma*.

**iman** (*i*, *man*; ou *man* avec *i* de liaison) m. noun expressing state or quality : *gariman* m. (*guru*) heaviness, *xépiman* (*xip*) swiftness.

**iya** (*ya*, avec *i* euphonique) m. and n. noun [f. *iyâ*] : *xétriya* m. n. (*xétra*) meadow; *ćintiyâ* f. (*ćint*) reflection.

**ira** (*ra*, avec *i* euphonique) : *agira* m. (*ag*) walker.

**ila** (*la*, avec *i* euphonique) adj. and m. noun [f. *ilâ*] *kalila* (*kal*) mixed, *kânila* (*kan*) m. hole-maker, *gatilâ* f. (*gata*) departure.

**ilya** (*ila*, *ya*).

**ivas** (*vas*) pluperfect

**iṣṭa** sfx. of the superlative.

**iṣṇu** *iṣṇulâ* (*snu*, avec *i* euph.)

इस् **is** n. noun *dyótis* (*dyut*) splendour; || adv. *vahis* (*vah*) without.

**iska** (*is*, *ka*) m. *mastiska* (*masta* tête) brain.

**isra** (*is*, *ra*) n. *tamisra* (*tamas*) darkness.

ई **î** (f. de *i*) *ucî* (*vać*) desire. [Sometimes *î* est simply lengthened from *i*].

**ika** (*î*, *ka*) *kaṇika* (*kaṇa*) small.

**ikin** (*ika*, *in*) *nâlikinî* f. (*nâlikā*) cluster of lotus flowers.

**ita** (*î*, *ta*) *nandita* m. (*nandi*) bald man.

**ina** (*î*, *na*) a. *grâmina* (*grâma*) of a village, *kulina* (*kula*) of good family.

**îya** (*î*; *ya*) a. *jâtîya* (*jâti*) generic.

**îyaka** (*îya*, *ka*) a.

**îyatâ** (*îya*, *tâ*) f. abstract de l'a. *îya*, corresponds to Fr. *ité*.

**îyas** sfx. of the comparative.

**îra** (*î*, *ra*) *kânḍîra* a. (*kânḍa*) armed with arrows.

**îśa** (*î*, *śa*) *târîśa* m. (*târâ*) the starry sky. || In the f. *îśâ* : *manîśâ* intelligence (*man*).

**îśin** (*îśa* ou *îśâ*, *in*) *manîśin* endowed with intelligence.

उ **u** forms m. and f. nouns *anću* (*anć*) piece, *iśu* m. f. (*iś*) arrow.

**uka** (*u*, *ka*) a. *gâmuka* (*gam*) who goes, *kâmuka* (*kam*) desirous.

**uṇa** (euph. for *una* : *u*, *na*) a. *dâruṇa* (*dṛ*) horrible.

**uṭa** (*u*, *ṭa*) *jaruṭa* n. (*jṛ*) leanness.

**ura** (*u*, *ra*) a. *ćatura* (*ćat*) cunning, *nisṭura* (*ni*, *stâ*) solid, fixed.

**ulu**; **ulîya**; **uléra**; (*u*, *la*, *u*; *u*, *la*, *îya*; etc.) *taṇḍulu* f. winnowed rice.

उस् **us** (euph. *uś*) *ćaxus* n. (*ćax*) eye, *janus* (*jan*) production. || Pour *vas* : *mîlhuś* (pour *mîḍwas* de *mih*) a. rainy.

## Right column

त *ta*

**uśa** (*us*, *a*) a. *kaluśa* (*kal*) mixed, confused.

**uśya** (*uśa*, *ya*) n. *væduśya* (*vidwas*, de *vid*) knowledge.

**û** (f. de *u*) often expresses the action considered abstractly : *karśû* (*kṛś*) agriculture.

**ûka** (*û*, *ka*) m. and f. noun *jâgarûka* m. (*jâgṛ*) watchman, *jalûkâ* f. (*jalu*) bat.

**ûla** (*û*, *la*) m. noun *kuçûla* (*kuça*) straw fire.

**énu** pour *énu* (*guna* de *ina*, *u*) *karénu* m. f. (*karin*) elephant.

**éya** (*guna* de *iya*) a. forming patronymics or expressing origin : *kâpéya* (*kapi*) of a monkey, *kæléya* (*kula*) of good family; || by ext. il exprime la possibility : *gaṇéya* (*gaṇa*) which can be counted.

**éra** (*guna* de *ira*) *danćéra* a. (*danć*) which bites.

**éruka** (*éra*, *uka* : *gaṇérukâ* f. procuress, *gaṅgérukî* f. species of sainfoin.

**éla** (*guna* de *ila*) s. *kraméla* m. (*kram*) camel. Cf. *îra*.

**élima** (*éla*, *ima*) a. *paćélima* (*pać*) made for cooking.

**éluka** (*éla*, *uka*) m. *paćéluka* (*pać*) cook. Cf. *éruka*.

**óla** (*guna* de *ula*) m. *gaḍóla* (*gaḍu*) mouthful.

**óli** (*óla*, *i*) f. *piṇḍóli* (*piṇḍa*) remains of a meal.

क **ka** adj. and noun *kalka* (*kal*) dirty, *kîlaka* m. (*kîla*) post.

**ku** (*ka*, *u*) dans le sfx. *âku*.

[ग **ga**, **gu** (*gam*, *u*) who goes].

च **ća**, dans *ćana* (*ća*, *na*) et dans *ćit* (*ća*, *it*) : *kadâćana*, *kalićit*.

**ḷa**, **ḷi**, **ḷu**, **ṇa**, euph. for *la*, *li*, *lu*, *na*.

त् **t** [in composition] *karmakṛt*, *prasénajit*, (*kṛ*, *ji*).

त **ta** sfx. of the past participle, which may be taken adjectivally and substantivally.

**tatâ** (*ta*, *tâ*) f. abstract of the preceding : *vićiṣṭatâ* (*vićiṣṭa*) distinction, state of a distinct thing.

**tana** (*ta*, *na*) a. [ordinarily indicating time] : *pragétana* (*pragé*) matutinal, *dóśâtana* (*dóśâ*) nocturnal.

**tama** (*ta*, *ma*) sfx. of the superlative.

**tara** (*ta*, *ra*; ou de *tṛ*) sfx. du comp.

**taya** (*ta*, *ya*) adj. and n. noun *tritaya* (*tri*) third, *dwitaya* n. (*dwi*) pair, couple.

**tavya** (*tu*, *ya*) a. *paṇitavya* (*paṇ*) dirtyable, *bhavitavya* (*bhû*) future.

**tavyatâ** (*tavya*, *tâ*) f. abstract du précé-
