# Sanskrit Roots, 6

[printed page number: 772]

## Left column

तुद् *tud*

कुथ् **kuth** to stink.

क्वथ् **kwath** to cook.

ग्रथ् **grath* *granth* *çranth* to be courbe, to knot, to combine.

तुन्थ् **tunth** to cover.

नाथ् **nâth* *nâdh* to be master.

प्रोथ् **próth** to neigh.

मथ् **math* *manth* *mánth* to agitate, to destroy; || *méth* *médh* to strike, to kill, cf. *mah*.

विथ् **vith* *viḍh* *véth* [*náth*] to ask.

व्यथ् **vyath** to suffer.

श्रथ् **çrath* *çranth* *çlath* to loosen, to rejoice.

अद् **ad** to eat.

उन्द् **und** to be master.

उन्द् **und* *kliḍ* to wet.

उर्द् **urd* *kurd* *khurd* *gurd* *guḍ* *gud* *ćṛd*, *krîḍ* (*krîl*) *kuḍ* *kaḍ* *kaṇḍ* *ćuḍḍ* *spuṇḍ* *spaṇḍ* *spuṇṭ* *spaṇṭ* to play (to taste).

कद् **kad* *kand* *krad* *krand* *klad* *kland* *klind* to cry; || *gad*, *vad* to speak; *ćad* to ask; *ćud* to question.

कर्द् **kard* *pard* *çṛd* to fart; *ćṛd* *ćard* to vomit; *had* to defecate.

खद् **khad* *skhad* *pad* *band* to be fixed et ferme.

क्लिद् **klid** to vex.

चन्द् **ćand* *ćṛd* to burn, to shine; *ćaṇḍ* to get angry; || *taṇḍ* to shine.

छिद् **ćhid* *bhid* *bhind* *bind* *skad* *çad* to split, to cut.

तुद् **tud* *tṛd* *tard* *skhad* to strike, to kill, cf. *at*; || *laḍ* *laṇḍ* to push; || *piḍ* to press.

त्रन्द् **trand* *tund* to strive.

## Right column

गाध् *gâdh*

नद् **nad** to resound, to cry; || *nand* to rejoice; || *nard* to bellow, *gard* to roar, cf. *kad*; || *nind* *nid* *néd* to blame.

नुद् **nud** to move (to hurl).

बुद् **bud* *bund* *ćund* *ćund* to perceive, to conceive; *budh* to know; *vid* to know (to find).

मद् **mad** to be drunk; || *mud* to be joyful; || *maṇḍ* *mṛḍ* *bhand* *bhaṇḍ* *pṛḍ* to rejoice; || *laḍ* *lal* to rejoice; || *méḍ* *mréḍ* *mléṭ* to be mad.

मृद् **mṛd* *mrad* *muṭ* *muḍ* *maṭ* *puḍ* *puṇḍ*, to crush, to grind.

रद् **rad** to split, to open.

रुद् **rud** to weep, to flow.

वन्द् **vand** to greet.

शब्द् **çabd** to say, to speak.

श्रद् **çrad** to have faith.

सद् **sad** to sit down.

स्कन्द् **skand** to go cf. *at*.

स्पन्द् **spand** to itch.

स्वद् **swad* *swâd* to taste good; *sward* to taste; *xwid* to love.

स्विद् **swid* *xwid* to sweat.

ह्राद् **hrâd** to resound.

ह्लाद् **hlâd** to rejoice.

अन्ध् **andh** to be blind.

इन्ध् **indh** to kindle.

ऋध् **ṛdh*, *vṛdh*, *idh*, to increase, to grow; || *vardh* to fill. Cf. *vṛh*.

क्रुध् **krudh** to get angry.

क्षुध् **xudh** to be hungry.

गाध् **gâdh** to persist.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
