# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 781]

**स्निह्** *snih*

[continued from p. 780]
*mańh, ruh* to grow; *varh* to be good, strong. cf. *ṛdh*.

**सह्** *sah suh* to endure, cf. *çak*.

**स्निह्** *snih* to love.

---

**स्रंह्** *srańh*

**स्नुह्** *snuh* to vomit.

**स्पृह्** *spṛh* to desire.

**स्रंह्** *srańh* to have confidence (*çrad*).

END.
