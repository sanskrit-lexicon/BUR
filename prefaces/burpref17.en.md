# Sanskrit Roots, 4

[printed page number: 770]

## PALATALS.

### Left column

शुच् *çuć*

अञ्च् **añć** to go (to honor); *aj* to go (to hurl); *añj* to go (to anoint), *arć*, *ṛć* to honor; *ij* *iñj* to go (to blame); *éj* to go (to tremble) || *añć*, *kać* *kuć* *kuñć* *kruñć* to go in a curve || *gruć* *gluć* *glunć* to go (to take); || *xañj* to go (to give); || *ćañć* to go (to stagger) || *ćarć* *jarć* *jarć* *jarć* *jarć* *jarj* *jarj* *jarj* to go toward (to speak à, to threaten, to strike, to apply oneself to): *juñć* to speak; || *tuñć* *twañć* to go (to press); || *dhwañj* *dhwaj*, *dhrañj* *dhraj*, *dhriñj* *dhṛñj* *dhṛj* to go (to move); || *mañć* *mać* *muñć* *muć* to circumvent || *mruć* *mluć* to go || *vañć* to go (vers, autour, beyond); *vyać* to circumvent; *vâñć* to desire; *vîj* to go (to blow); *vrañj* *vraj* to go (to go away); || *swañj* to embrace || *sañć* *sañj* *sajj* to go (to move). Cf. *ak*.

उच् **uć** to be fit, to suit.

कच् **kać* *kańć* to shine, to cry. || *kuć* to resound, *kuñj* to murmur, *kûj* to peep, to moan, *xij* to moan; || *gaj* *garj* *gṛj* *gṛñj* to bellow, to roar; *gañj* *guj* to resound; || *jarj* *jañj* to fight; *tarj* to quarrel; || *mañj* *miñj* *maj* *muñj* *muj*, *mṛñj* *mṛj*, *marć* *mûrj*, *marv* to resound, to shine, to speak; || *bhrâj* *râj* to shine (to reign); || *ruć* *luñj* *lóć*, to shine, to speak; || *vać* to say || *varć* to shine; *vić* to shine, to speak (to go toward); || *çać* to say, to speak; || *çuć* to shine; || *spurj* to resound (to thunder). Cf. *kâç*.

कच् **kać* *kańć* *kâć*, *ûć*, to bind; || *tañć* *tañj* to contract.

त्वच् **twać** to cover (with a skin).

पच् **pać** to cook.

पच् **pać* *pañć* to develop; || *pṛć* *pṛñć*, *pṛj* *pṛñj* to spread. Cf. *vṛś*.

मुच् **muć** to untie.

याच् **yâć** (to go toward) to ask, to offer.

राच् **râć** to prepare.

### Right column

कर्ज् *karj*

सच् **sać* *saćć* to follow. Cf. *añć*.

सिच् **sić** to sprinkle.

सूच् **sûć** to reveal.

स्तुच् **stuć** to be propitious.

आञ्छ् **âñćh** to lengthen.

उञ्छ् **uñćh** to glean.

ऊछ् **ûćh** to go beyond, to leave; || *ûjh* to leave.

ऋछ् **ṛćh** to freeze.

प्रछ् **praćh** to address.

मिछ् **mićh* *pićh* to torment.

मुर्छ् **murćh** to be vehement.

म्लेछ् **mléćh** to speak obscurely.

यछ् **yaćh** cf. *yam*.

युछ् **yućh* *pućh* *mućh* to be negligent.

लछ् **laćh* *lâñćh* to note (cf. *lax*).

स्फुर्छ् **spurćh* *swurćh* to forget.

हुर्छ् **hurćh** to go in a curve.

ह्रीछ् **hrićh** to be ashamed (cf. *hrî*).

अर्ज् **arj* *sarj* to do/make.

उब्ज् **ubj** to make straight.
