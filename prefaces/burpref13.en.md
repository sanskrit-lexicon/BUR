# Sanskrit Suffixes, 5

[printed page number: 765]

## Left column

व *va*

र **ra** a. possessive : *kuñjara* m. elephant (*kuñja* tusk), *garvara* (*garva*) proud; || S. m. *ćandra* (*ćand*) the moon.

**ru** (*ra*, *u*) a. *dâru* (*dâ*) liberal, *bhîru* (*bhî*) fearful.

**rhi** (*ra*, *hi* ?) adv. *tarhi* therefore.

ल **la** a. (cf. *ra*) : *grantila* (*granta*) knotty. || *la* pour *ra*, dans le Véda : *xulla* pour *xudra*, small.

व **va** adj. and noun indicating possession or origin : *kéćava* (*kéća*) who has beautiful hair; || *va* also forms des adverbs of manner : *iva* like, *éva* thus; || *va* may proceed from *u*.

**vat** (*va*, *at*; ou *u*, *at*) forms a great number of qualifying adjectives; || et des adv. of similarity : *ćandravats* like the moon.

**van** (*va*, *an*; ou *u*, *an*) m. noun *nitwan* the wind.

**vara** (*va*, *ra*) a. *bhâswara* (*bhâs*) brilliant, *tuvara* (*tu*) astringent; || *vara* is analogous to *ura*.

**vala** a. (*va*, *la*) *çikâvala* (*çikâ*) which ends in a point. || This sfx. is analogous to *vara* et à *ula*, *ura*.

**vas** (*va*, *as*) sfx. of the pluperfect *jujurvas* (*jṛ*) become old.

**vi** (*va*, *i*) m. noun *jâgṛvi* m. watchman. *kṛvi* weaver's craft. || *vî* is the feminine of *vi*.

## Right column

हि *hi*

…et de *u* : *urvî* (*uru*), *pṛthwî* et *pṛtivî* (*pṛthu*).

**vya** (*va*, *ya*) *bhrâtṛvya* brother's son (*bhrâtṛ*).

श **ça** sfx. of qualifying adjectives analogous to *sa* : *rómaça* (*róman*) hairy, *karkaça* hard, harsh.

**ças** adv. *kaṇḍaças* (*kaṇḍa*) in pieces.

ष **śa** euph. for *sa* : *puruśa*.

**śṇa**, **śṇu**, euph. for *sna*, *snu* : *géśṇa* *géśṇu* (*gæ*) singer.

स् **s** et *iś* sfx. of desideratives.

स **sa** a. *tṛṇasa* (*tṛṇa*) of grass. || In the f. *sâ* sfx. of desire : *ćikitsâ* (*kit*).

**san** (*sa*, *an*) m. noun *uxan* (*vah*) bull.

**su** (*s*, *u*) a. désidératifs : *jijñâsu* (*jñâ*) who desires to know.

**stât** (ou *tât*) form of adverbial ablative : *parastât* beyond.

**sna** (*s* ou *sa*, *na*) *tixṇa* a. (*tij*) penetrating. || In the f. *snâ* et *snî* : *jyôtsnâ*, *jyôtsnî*, (*jyótis*) moonlight.

**snu** (*sna*, *u*) a. *glâsnu* (*glæ*) weary.

**swit** sfx. of adv. *kiṅswit* or perhaps.

हि **hi** adv. *karhi* when? cf. *rhi*. || This sfx. also forms the 2nd p. sg. of certain imperatives : *âpnuhi*. || In both these cases, its ancient form is *dhi*.
