# Sanskrit Suffixes, 4

[printed page number: 764]

## Left column

ध *dha*

…dent : *bhavitavyatâ* futureity, possibility of happening.

**tas** (*ta*, *as*; ou *as* avec *t* euph.) *rétas* n. (*ri*) seed/semen. || This sfx. also forms adv. : *kutas*, whence?

**tâ** (f. de *ta*) sfx. of abstract words, indicating quality, state, collection : *grâmatâ* (*grâma*) collection of villages; || it also expresses the action : *xutâ* (*xu*) sneeze.

**ti** (*ta*, *i*) f. noun expressing an active quality or an action : *âpti* (*âp*) acquisition, *xânti* (*xam*) patience, *âyati* (*yam*) extension.

**tika** (*ti*, *ka*) : *mṛttikâ* (*mṛt*) clay, earth.

**tin** (*ta*, *in*) m. expressing the agent : *ghâtin* (*ghâta*, de *han*) murderer.

**timat** (*ti*, *mat*) a. expressing the quality signified by *ti* : *vibhûtimat* excellent.

**tiya** (*ta*, *iya*) a. *tritiya* (*trita*) third.

**tîyîka** (*tiya*, *îka*) a. *dwætîyîka* (*dwi*) which concerns the second.

**tu** (*ta*, *u*) : *aktu* f. (*añj*) smeared thing, *ṛtu* (*ṛ*) season. || The acc. *tum* is the sfx. of the infinitive

**tulâ** (*tu*, *la*) f. noun *jantulâ* species of reed.

तृ **tṛ** (*ta*, *r*; analogous to *tu* and perhaps the root *tṛ*) sfx. of agent nouns : *kartṛ* (*kṛ*) he who does, *dâtṛ* he who gives; a. *xantṛ* (*xam*) patient; || sfx. of the futuree : *bhavitṛ* (*bhû*) who will be.

**tṛtwa** (*tṛ*, *twa*) n. noun of abstract activity : *kartṛtwa* activity, *pitṛtwa* paternity.

**tnu** (*ta*, *nu*) m. noun *kṛtnu* (*kṛ*) workman, *madayitnu* (*mad* 10) drunken man.

**tya** (*ta*, *ya*) adj. and pass. part. *kṛtya* which is to be done, *nitya* perpetual.

**tyu** (*tya*, *u*) m. noun f. n. *mṛtyu* (*mṛ*) death.

**tra** (*tṛ*, *a*) adj. and n. noun denoting the instrument : *xâttra* a. royal; *kanitra* n. (*kan*) hoe; *xétra* n. (*xi*) field. || This sfx. also forms adv. : *tatra*, *yatra*, etc.

**trin** (*tra*, *in*) m. noun [f. *trinî*] : *patatrin* bird (*patatra* aile).

**trima** (*tra*, *ima*) a. *kṛtrima* (*kṛ*) artificial.

**trî** (f. de *tṛ*) *gantrî* (*gam*) chariot.

**trya** (*tṛ* ou *tra*, *ya*) a. *pitrya* (*pitṛ*) paternal.

**twa** (*tu*, *a*) s. expressing the quality abstraite.

## Right column

य *ya*

…en deux. || **dha**, pour *ta*, après un *ḍ* : *ṛḍḍha* pour *ṛḍta* (*ṛḍ*).

**dhi** pour *ti* après un *ḍ* : *ṛḍḍhi* pour *ṛḍti*. || *dhi* is also the 2nd p. sg. imp. Ved.

**dhṛ** pour *tṛ* after an aspirate : *dógdhṛ* pour *dóhtṛ* (*duh*).

न **na** adj. and pass. part.

**natwa** (*na*, *twa*) *nagnatwa* n. (*naj*) nudity.

**ni** (*na*, *i*) *glâni* f. (*glæ*) weariness.

**nin** (*na*, *in*) *dhûnin* m. (*dhû*) one of the Maruts.

**nu** (*na*, *u*) a. *çaknu* who can speak.

**nutâ** (*nu*, *tâ*) f. noun abstract : *gṛdhnutâ* covetousness (*gṛdhnu* desirous).

भ **bha** (from the root *bhâ*) a. which has the appearance of.

म **ma** adj. and m. noun n. : *jihma* winding, *xéma* happy, *jâlma* rash, *kárma* (*karman*) laborious; *iśma* m. (*iś*) desire; *karma* n. (*kṛ*) action.

**mat** (*ma*, *at*) a. *garutmat* (*garut*) winged.

**man** (*ma*, *an*) m. n. noun *uśman* m. (*uś*) heat; *karman* n. (*kṛ*) work.

**maya** (*ma*, *ya*; ou *mi*, *a*) adj. and noun expressing nature or origin : *gómaya* (*gó*) of the nature of a cow; n. noun cow-dung.

**mala** (*ma*, *la*) a. *dhûmala* (*dhû*) smoke-coloured.

**maśa** (*ma*, *śa*) *kalmaśa* (*kal*) a. soiled, dirty.

**mâśa** (*ma* in the f., *śa*) a. *kalmâśa* soiled, stained.

**mi** (*ma*, *i*) m. and f. noun : *dalmi* m. (*dal*) thunderbolt; *bhûmi* f. the earth; *némi*, *némi*, f. (*nî*) wheel-rim.

**min** (*ma*, *in*) [f. *minî*] adj. and noun *xémin* a. (*xéma*) happy; *ćarmin* a. (*ćarma*) who has a shield; *gulminî* f. (*gulma*) bushy place.

**mra**, **mya**, pour *mara*, *maya* : *dhûmra*, *dhûmyâ*.

य **ya** sfx. of the passive participle expressing obligation, necessity or mere possibility : *kârya* to be done, feasible (*kṛ*). || Sfx. d'a. *vanya* (*vana*) of the woods, *gavya* (*gó*) of an ox. || S. m. *âmnâya* (*â*; *mnâ*) thing that must be remembered. || S. n. abstract quality; *asurya* n. quality of an *asura*.
