# SANSKRIT SUFFIXES (continued)

*(Alphabetical list of suffixes — two columns)*

---

**अत्** *at* pres. part. (strong cases, *ant*).

*ata* (*a*, *ta*) adj. and m. n. *tamata* (*tam*) who desires, *marata* (*mṛ*) death.

*ati* (*a*, *ti*) f. n. *casati* (*cas*) weakening. In the f. *atî*: *tālatī* (*tāla*) palm wine.

*atu* (*a*, *tu*) m. n. *xaṇatu* (*xaṇ*) blow.

*atka* (*at*, *ka*) adj. *cañcatka* (*cuñc*) tottering.

*aṭa* (*a*, *ṭa*) m. n. *klamaṭa* (*klama*) fatigue.

*aṭu* (*a*, *ṭu*) m. n. *davaṭu* (*dava*) anxiety.

**अद्** *ad* for *at*: *tarad* (*tṛ*) raft, *basad* (*bas*) sun.

**अन्** *an* m. n. *taxan* (*tax*) maker, *ahan* the day.

*ana* (*a*, *na*) adj. *kamana* (*kam*) loving, lovable; ‖ m. n. *kādana* (*kād*) tooth; ‖ ordinarily in the n., the action done or being done, often with a causative value. ‖ In the f. *anā*: *gaṇanā* calculation.

*anaka* (*ana*, *ka*).

*anyaka* (*ana*, *ya*, *ka*).

*ani* (*ana*, *i*) f. n. *janani* production.

*anika* (*ana*, *ika*) *lekhaniika* (*lekha*) letter-carrier.

*anīya* (*ana*, *īya*) adj. and fut. pass. part. in the sense of Latin *bilis* or *dus da dum*: *gaṇanīya* that can be counted.

*anīyaka* (*ana*; *īya*, *ka*) same meaning.

*anta* (strong stem of *at*; *a*) adj. *jīvanta* (*jīv*) living.

*anyatas* (*ana*, *ya*, *tas*) adv. *pradhānyatas* (*dhā*) summarily.

*abha* (*a*, *bha*) m. n. *rāsabha* (*rāsa*) ass.

**अम्** *am* suffix of pronouns and pronominal adjectives: *aham*, *idam*, etc.

*ama* (*a*, *ma*) adj. *carama* (*car*) final.

*aya* (*a*, *ya*) adj. and n. *gavaya* (*go*) species of ox. ‖ The sfx. *aya* forms the 10th class of verbs and the causative verbs, where it appears to be a development of the root *i*, to go.

*ayanta* (pres. part. of *aya* caus.) adj. and m. n. [f. *ī*]: *gaḍayanta* (*gaḍ*) rain-cloud.

*ayitṛ* (*aya* caus., *tṛ*) *janayitṛ* he who begets.

**अर्** *ar* n. *ahar* day; cf. *as*.

*ara* (*a*, *ra*) cf. *ra*: *cīvara* rag.

*arin* (*ara*, *in*) adj. and m. n. *cīvarin* covered with a rag.

*arya* (*ara*, *ya*) n. *maekarya* (*muekara*) impertinence.

*ala* (*a*, *la*) adj. *capala* (*cap*) wavering.

*alaka* (*ala*, *ka*) m. *cagalaka* he-goat.

*avya* (*u*, *ya*) *paśavya* (*paśu*) proper to herds.

**अस्** *as* m. f. n.: *cakṣas* (*cakṣ*) m. insti-

*(right column, continued derivatives in ā- and i-)*

*asa* (*as*, *a*) adj. and m. n. n. *tāmasa* (*tamas*) dark, *camasa* m. sacred cup, *tamasa* n. darkness.

*asti* (*as*, *ti*) *gabhasti* (for *grahasti*) hand, organ of prehension.

*asya* (*as*, *ya*) adj. *tapasya* (*tapas*) penitent.

*asvin* (*as*, *vin*) adj. *tapasvin* (*tapas*) devout.

**ā** f. of *a*; cf. *a*.

*āku* (*ā*, *ku*) adj. and n. *kuśāku* (*kuś*) fire, the Sun.

*ātu* (*ā*, *tu*) *jīvātu* (*jīv*) food.

*āna* sfx. identical with sfx. *ana*; ‖ it also forms participles; cf. M § 93.

*ānika* (*āna*, *ika*) *galānika* m. (*gala*) shrimp.

*ānila* (*āna*, *ila*) *galānila* m. (*gala*) shrimp.

*āyu* (*ā*, *yu*) *vātāyu* m. antelope.

*āra* (*ā*, *ra*) *madāra* m. (*mad*) elephant in rut.

*āru* (*ā*, *ru*) *kuṭāru* m. (*kuṭa*) tree.

*āla* (*ā*, *la*) *celāla* m. (*cela*) cucumber.

*ālaka* (*āla*, *ka*) *kurālaka* (*kur*) m. arrow.

*ālika* (*āla*, *ika*) *kurālika* (*kura*) m. barber’s shop.

*ālu* (*ā*, *lu*) adj. and n. *kṛpālu* (*kṛpā*) compassionate, *pakṣālu* (*pakṣa*) bird.

*āvila* (*ā*, *ava* from *u*. *ila*) *galāvila* (*gala*) shrimp.

**इ** *i*. Preceded by *ā*, it suppresses that vowel: *abdhi* (*ābd*). The sea. It forms adjectives and m. nouns expressing the agent: *kavi* (*kav*) poet, singer; *akṣi* eye (*akṣa*).

*ika* (*i*, *ka*; or *ka* preceded by a linking *i*) adj. and m. and n. n. [f. *ikā*]: *kulika* (*kula*) of good family, *kucika* (*kuśa*) m. ploughshare; *gaṇikā* f. (*gaṇ*) calculation, *corikā* (*cora*) thief’s trade.

*ikatā* (*ika*, *tā*) abstract f. of the preceding sfx.: *nāstikatā* atheism.

**इज्** *ij* (perhaps from the root *yaj*) *baṇij* (*baṇ*) merchant, *ṛtvij* (*ṛtu*) priest.

**इत्** *it* f. n. *taḍit* (*taḍ*) lightning.

*ita* (*i*, *ta*; or *ta* preceded by a linking *i*) forms past participles, adjectives, and n. nouns. ‖ It often indicates the past participle of a causative verb.

*itṛ*, *itra*, *itrī*, cf. *tṛ*, *tra*.

*itya* (*tya* with euphonic *i*) *cayitya* (*cay*) n. sacred monument.

**इन्** *in* [f. *inī*] adj. and m. and f. n. *kāmin* (*kāma*) who loves, *karin* m. (*kara*) elephant, *kamalinī* f. (*kamala*) collection of lotuses.

*ina* (*in*, *a*) n. n. *guhina* (*guh*) thicket, *carmina* (*carman*) assembly of men having shields.

*ineya* (*ina* or *ini*, *eya*).
