# SANSKRIT ROOTS

GROUPED AND CLASSIFIED ACCORDING TO THEIR FINAL LETTERS
AND THEIR PRINCIPAL ANALOGIES.

[printed page number: 767]

## VOWELS.

### Left column

ग **gâ** (cf. *gam*) to go; || *yâ*, to go; || *hâ* to go away, to leave, *hi* to go (to hurl).

ज्ञा **jñâ** to know.

दा **dâ** to give; Ved. *râ*.

धा **dhâ** to put.

ध्मा **dhmâ** to blow.

पा **pâ**, *pî*, to drink; *dhé* to drink, to suck.

पा **pâ** to protect.

प्सा **psâ** to eat.

भा **bhâ** to shine, cf. *di* and *kâç*.

मा **mâ**, *mi*, to measure.

मा **mâ** to resound.

म्ना **mnâ** (*man*) to make mention.

ला **lâ** to take.

वा **vâ** to blow.

स्था **sthâ** to stand.

इ **i**, *vî* to go; || *ṛ*, *sṛ*; *ri*, *rî*, (*ḍî* voler) *çri* to go; *çyæ* to move; || *su* to go; *sru*, *çru*, *snu*, couler (*snâ* laver); || *klu*, *pru* to go à (atteindre); *plu* naviguer; || *xi* to go à, to dwell. Cf. *av*.

क्षि **xi** to be master.

चि **ći** to gather; || *ki* to know.

### Right column

ज्रि **jri**, *jrî*, *jyâ* to grow old; || *glæ* to languish.

पि **pi**, *pyæ*, to fatten.

मि **mi** to throw.

शि **śi**, *śó*, *xṇu*, to sharpen.

च्वि **ćwi** to grow fat.

सि **si** to bind.

स्मि **smi** to smile.

क्री **krî** to buy.

नी **nî**, *nṛ*, to lead.

प्री **prî** to rejoice (cf. *pṛ*).

भी **bhî**, *bhrî*, to fear.

मी **mî** to pass through.

ली **lî** to dissolve.

ल्वी **lwî**, *lpî* [*lî*] to join oneself to s.o.

शी **śî** to lie down.

ह्री **hrî** (*hrić*) to be ashamed.

उ कु **u**, *ku*, *kû*, to resound, to cry; || *knu*, *knú*, to resound, to cry; *xu* to sneeze; || *ku* to resound; *kyâ*, *hwé* to call; || *gu*, *gú* to resound; *gâ*, *gæ*, *gṛ* to sing; *kæ* to croak; || *ru* to cry, *ræ* to bark; || *swṛ* to resound. || *nu*, *nû*, *stu*, to praise [sometimes to thunder]; *styæ*, *śîyæ*, to cry.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
