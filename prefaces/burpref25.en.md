# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 778]

**इष्** *iṣ*

**शिल्** *çil sil* to glean.

**शील्** *çīl* faire, to meditate, to worship.

**शूल्** *çūl* to cry out, to be ill cf. *jwar [ćar]*.

## SIBILANTS.

**अष्** *aç ax iś* to occupy a space.

**अश्** *aç* to eat.

**ईश्** *īç* to be master.

**कष्** *kaç* to resound, cf. *ras*.

**कृष्** *kṛç hras hlas liç* to be or make small.

**क्लिश्** *kliç* to torment, *klēç* to wound.

**दंश्** *dańç dańs* to bite.

**दिश्** *diç* to show.

**दृश्** *dṛç* to see.

**नश्** *naç das las* to perish; *dāç dās* to kill.

**निश्** *niç* to meditate.

**पश्** *paç paś pas spaç* to bind, to attach.

**पिश्** *piç* to figure, cf. *piñj*.

**भ्रंश्** *bhrańç bhraç bhṛç bhrāńs dhwańs srańs srańs* to fall.

**वश्** *vaç vāś laś* to desire.

**वृष्** *vṛç vṛx* to choose, to cover, cf. *vṛ*.

**स्पृश्** *spaç spṛç mṛç* to touch.

**इष्** *iṣ* to desire; || to reiterate.

---

**तुष्** *tuṣ*

**स्थल्** *sthal (sthā)* to be firm.

**हल्** *hal (khan)* to plough.

**हिल्** *hil* to express one's will; (to frolic, cf. *kil*).

## SIBILANTS.

**इष्** *iṣ* to go, to strike; *ēṣ ṛṣ* to go, cf. *ṛ*; || *ṛx riṣ ruṣ riç ruc, yuṣ yūṣ, juṣ jūṣ jaṣ jas, baṣ bhiç bhēç piç pēṣ viṣ vēṣ vaṣ, pańç pańs maṣ muṣ mus vriṣ vrūṣ bhrūṣ, çaṣ çās snaṣ kaṣ ćiṣ hiṅs* to strike, to wound, to kill.

**ईक्ष्** *īx (ax) vēx vlēx lax* to look, to notice; *ćāx* to see; *sūrx (su, lax)* to observe.

**उष्** *uṣ* to burn, to shine; *twiṣ tańs* to shine, to adorn; *ḍūṣ ḍūs duç, rūṣ lūṣ luṣ*; to adorn, to adorn; *ḍux ḍix* to kindle (desid. of *dah*); *bhiṣ, ǵuṣ ǵāṣ ǵāńs* to embellish; *vyuṣ byus pyuṣ, bus pus puṣ* to burn; *çuṣ* to dry up.

**ऊष्** *ūṣ* to be ill.

**काङ्क्ष्** *kāṅx* (desid. of *kam*), *drāṅx māṅx vāṅx* to desire.

**कृष्** *kṛṣ kuṣ* to tear out.

**गवेष्** *gavēṣ (gō iṣ) gēṣ glēṣ* to search.

**घंष्** *ǵhańṣ ǵhańs* to drip.

**घृष्** *ǵṛṣ* to grind, to rub.

**चूष्** *ćūṣ* to suck.

**जुष्** *juṣ* to love.

**तक्ष्** *tax twax jaṣ ṛṣ ūṣ* to take, to cover.

**तुष्** *tuṣ tūṣ* to rejoice.
