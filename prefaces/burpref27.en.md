# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 780]

**ग्रह्** *grah*

**शंस्** *çańs* to narrate.

**श्वस्** *çwas* to breathe.

**शस्** *ças sas çańst sańst* to sleep.

**शास्** *çās* to govern.

---

## ASPIRATE.

**अर्ह्** *arh* to be worthy; *rih* to offer worship, to praise; cf. *arǵ*.

**अह्** *ah* to say.

**अह्** *ah aḍ* to increase.

**अंह्** *ańh* to go; *rańh vańh* to go, to run; || *rah* to go away, leave; Cf. *ak añć*.

**ईह्** *īh* tendre vers; *jēh vēh bēh* to strive toward.

**उह्** *uh luh duh* to torment; *druh* vouloir to harm; *tṛh stṛh stṛh* to strike, to kill. Cf. *tud*.

**ऊह्** *ūh* to conceive, to understand (*vah*).

**कुह्** *kuh* to admire, to astonish.

**गह्** *gah* to understand with difficulty.

**गर्ह्** *garh galh* to blame, cf. *galbh*.

**गाह्** *gāh* to penetrate.

---

**वृह्** *vṛh*

**चह्** *ćah* to deceive.

**दह्** *dah dańh* to burn.

**दिह्** *dih* to anoint.

**दुह्** *duh* to milk.

**द्राह्** *drāh* to awaken.

**नह्** *nah* to knot.

**प्लिह्** *plih* to go, to move.

**बर्ह्** *barh varh mańh* to shine, to speak. cf. *kać*; to strike, to kill; || *mah* to immolate.

**माह्** *māh* to measure, cf. *mas*.

**मिह्** *mih* to spread, cf. *pṛṣ*.

**मुह्** *muh* to have a troubled mind.

**लिह्** *lih* to lick.

**लुह्** *luh* to desire, cf. *lubh rabh*.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
