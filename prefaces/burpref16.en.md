2nd *Aṅg* to go; *aṅk* (*aṅg*) to mark; *aṅg* *ag* to go in a curve (to fish), *iṅg* to go (to move). || *kag* to go || *laṅg*, *tig*, *traṅg* *twaṅg* to go (to move) || *maṅg* to go (to walk) *mṛg* *márg* (to pursue) *maṅg* to go (to begin, to blame, to circumvent) || *raṅg* *riṅg* *raṅg* to go (to hurry) || *laṅg* to go (to limp) *liṅg* to go (to make a grammatical inflection) *laṅg* to cross || *vaṅg* to limp; *vaṅg*, cf. *maṅg*; *valg* to go by leaps || *çraṅg* *çlaṅg* *ćwaṅg* *swaṅg* to move.

चक् **ćak** to resist, to endure. || *ćikk* *ćakk* *ćukk* to make suffer. || *ćîk* to endure. || *hikk* *hiśk* *kiśk* to kill. || *ḍhakk* *nakk* to kill. || *ćag* *tig* *dag* *sag* to kill.

तङ्क् **taṅk** to be unhappy.

द्रेक् **drék* *dhrék* to grow (to shout for joy, to be proud); Cf. *dṛh*.

लोक् **lók* *ḍék* to see; || *rék* *rag* *lag* to suspect. || *vaśk* *viśk* to see.
