# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 775]

**जप्** *jap*

## LABIALS with V (व).

**आप्** *āp* to acquire.

**अब्**, **अर्ब्**, **अम्**, **अम्भ्** *ab, arb, am, ambh*,
**अव्**, **अर्व्**, **इन्व्**, **उर्व्** *av, arv, inv, urv*, to go.

1° *gam* to go, *dram* to run, *hamm hrīp* to go; || *kamb karb kharb garb, ćamp ćāmp ćarb, parp*, to go; || *kram* to walk, *bhram* to wander; || *klam, çram* to be weary, *çam* to rest.

2° *av danv* to go, *ḍanv ḍāv* to run, to flee; *çav* to go (to lose);

3° *ćup* to go slowly; *kūp kṛp* to be weak, *klib kliv* to be powerless; || *krap kap* to go (to have pity);

4° *kamp kēp gēp glēp vēp* to go (to tremble, to waver); *xubh* to be shaken.

5° *am* to go (to resound), *nam mim syam ambh rambh lambh ribh rēp*, to resound.

6° *inv rinv rimbh ranv rambh* to go; *rēv* to jump; *pinv minv* to spread, *ninv* to water; || *kēv gēv glēv, pēv pēbh plēv, mēv mēbh mlēv mēp, lēp, çēv sēv*, to worship, to serve; *sap* to worship (to follow).

7° *arv urv, jambh jurv, tarb tamb, tup tump tuph lumph tubh tumbh dambh nabh trup trump truph trumph turv thurv durv ḍurv dṛp dṛph dṛmph, ṛp ṛmph rimph raph ramph rarh, lumb, çambh çarbh çarv, sambh sarbh sarv, sṛbh simbh sibh srimbh sribh*, to go against, to strike, to kill, to torment.

**कुप्** *kup* (to shine) to be irritated. || *çubh çumbh subh sumbh* to shine (to speak; to strike, to kill).

**कुम्प्** *kump kumb kumbh*, to cover, *gup* to protect.

**कृप्** *kḷip* to cause to become.

**चप्** *xap xip ḍabh ḍambh ḍimbh ḍamb ḍimb ḍip ḷip slēp* to throw, to hurl.

**चप्** *ćap* to calm, to deceive, to crush.

**चुप्** *ćup ćhup* to touch.

**चृप्** *ćṛp ćhp dṛp* to lighten.

**जप्** *jap* to speak low, *jalp* to say (to praise); || *klap hlap hrap* to speak (to crack); *rap* to say (to praise); *lap* to speak (to lament); || *çap* to curse.

**गल्भ्** *galbh*

**दिम्प्** *dimp dṛmp dambh dimbh ḍamp ḍimp ḍambh ḍimbh ḍap ḍip slūp* to amass, to accumulate.

**तप्** *tap* to burn, *dīp* to be burning.

**तृप्** *tṛp tṛmp tṛph tṛmph dṛp* to be content, satisfied.

**त्रप्** *trap* to be ashamed.

**धूप्** *dhūp* to smoke.

**पुष्प्** *puṣp* to flower.

**युप्** *yup rup* to disturb, to violate.

**रूप्** *rūp* to form.

**लिप्** *lip* to smear.

**लुप्** *lup* to break.

**वप्** *vap* to sow.

**शुर्प्** *çurp çulbh* to measure.

**स्तिप्** *stip stēp lip lēp dīp dēp slim lim tīm* to be wet, to drip.

**स्वप्** *swap sup* to sleep.

**कब्** *kab kav* cf. *ku*.

**डम्भ्** *ḍambh* to imitate.

**शम्ब्** *çambh sambh sāmbh* to bind together; || *ćumbh* to kiss; || *jambh jabh yabh* to have carnal intercourse.

**उभ्** *ubh umbh* to fill.

**गल्भ्** *galbh karv kharv garv xibh xiv ćibh bibh çibh* to be drunk (with pride).
