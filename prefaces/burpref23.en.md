# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 776]

**चुच्य्** *ćućy*

**जम्भ्** *jabh jambh jṛbh jṛmbh* to yawn.

**डृभ्** *ḍṛbh* to knot, to arrange.

**रभ्** *rabh lubh* to desire intensely.

**लभ्** *labh* to attain, to obtain.

**श्रम्भ्** *çrambh srambh* to entrust oneself to someone. cf. *srańh* et *çrad*.

**स्तम्भ्** *stambh ṣṭambh skabh skumbh* to stop, to prop, to consolidate; *stubh stumbh* to render motionless with astonishment.

**कम्** *kam* to love, to desire, cf. *kram*; || *ćam ćām* to eat, to drink; *ćarv, bharv bharbh valbh* to eat.

**चम्** *xam* to endure; *xamp* to bear.

**तम्** *tam* to pine with grief.

**दम्** *dam yam* to tame, to restrain.

**नम्** *nam* to bend.

**भाम्** *bhām* to be irritated, cf. *bhā* et *kup*.

**रम्** *ram* to take pleasure, to rejoice.

**वम्** *vam* to vomit.

**शम्** *śam slam* to be [or not be] troubled.

**साम्** *sām* to conciliate (denomin. of *sama*).

**खव्** *khav* to be a ghost.

**गुर्व्** *gurv* to help.

**चीव्** *ćīv ćībh ćīy rīv* to cover, to take.

**जीव्** *jīv* to live.

**तीव्** *tīv nīv pīv mīv* to become fat.

**तेव्** *tēv dēv* to moan.

**दिव्** *div* to shine, to rejoice, to play; *dinv dēv ḍinv hinv jinv* to rejoice, to play.

**धाव्** *dhāv* to wash.

**पर्व्** *parv purv pūrv pūrbh bharv bharbh* to fill cf. *pūr pṝ*.

**मुर्व्** *murv* to bind, to knot.

**ष्ठीव्** *ṣṭhīv ṣṭīv* to spit.

**सिव्** *siv* to sew.

**स्रिव्** *sriv (sṛ et sru)* to go, to dry up.

## SEMIVOWELS य्, र्, ल्, Y R L.

**अय्** *ay* cf. *i*.

**ईर्ष्य्** *īrṣy īrśy sūrṣy* to envy.

**ऊय्** *ūy* cf. *vē* to weave.

**क्नूय्** *knūy* to resound cf. *swan*.

**चीय्** *ćīy* cf. *ćīv*.

**चुच्य्** *ćućy ṣućy* to extract, cf. *su*.

**दाय्** *dāy* cf. *dā*.

**पूय्** *pūy knūy* to stink (to be or become moist).

**स्फाय्** *sphāy sthūl* to become fat.

**अह्र्** *ahr* to go, to wander; || *vahr bahr mahr* to go; || *swahr* to go (to break, to be miserable).

**कत्र्** *katr gātr khart kartr* to loosen, to untie.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
