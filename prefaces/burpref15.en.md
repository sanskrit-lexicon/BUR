# Sanskrit Roots, 2

[printed page number: 768]

## Left column

धृ *dhṛ*

गु **gu** to defecate.

तु **tu** [to go] to wound, cf. *tud*; || *du* to make suffer; to push aside.

जु **ju**, *jyu*, *jyu*, *ćyu*, to go, to hurry; || *ćyu* to fall. || Cf. *u*, *ku*.

द्यु **dyu**, *jyu*, *di*, *dîdî*, to shine [*div*].

द्रु **dru**, *dhru*, to go, to run; || to wound, to kill. || *dhu*, *dhû* to agitate; || *hu* to kill, to immolate.

यु **yu** to bind [*yuj*].

यु **yu** to prevent, to push aside.

श्रु **çru** to hear.

सु **su**, *sû*, to extract a juice; || to give birth.

स्कु **sku** to cover.

पू **pû** to purify.

ब्रू **brû** to say.

भू **bhû** to be.

मू **mû** to bind.

सू **sû** to excite.

कृ **kṛ** to do/make.

कृ **kṛ**, *kṛ*, to cut, to kill; || *jṛ*, *ji* to conquer; || *xi* to kill; *xæ*, *jæ*, *sæ*, to destroy oneself; || *ćó* *só*, *cṛ*, *swṛ*, to cut to pieces; || *dṛ* to lacerate; *di* to destroy; *dâ*, *dó*, *lû*, to cut.

दृ **dṛ** to look at (to honor); || *dyæ* to despise.

द्वृ **dwṛ**, वृ **vṛ**, *vyé*, to cover.

धृ **dhṛ** *dhi*, *dhi*, to hold, to carry; || *bhṛ*, *bhṛ*, to carry; || *hṛ* to take, *hnu* to steal.

## Right column

ज्यो *jyó*

ध्वृ **dhwṛ** to bend; || *hwṛ* to be bent.

पृ **pṛ** to be busy with.

मृ **mṛ**, *mṛ*, to die; || *mlæ* to wither.

वृ **vṛ**, *vri*, *vli*, to choose.

शृ **çṛ**, *çri*, *çræ*, *çrâ*, to cook.

स्तृ **stṛ**, *stṛ*, to stretch; || *slæ* to clothe, to adorn.

स्मृ **smṛ** to remember.

स्वृ **swṛ** (*swar*) to sound, cf. *swan*.

कृ **kṛ** to spread; || *gṛ*, *gṛ*, to spread (to shine).

कृ **kṛ** to know, to judge.

गृ **gṛ** to swallow; || *ghrâ* to smell.

जृ **jṛ**, *jṛ*, *dhṛ*, *jâgṛ*, to watch.

तृ **tṛ** to cross; || *træ* to save.

पृ **pṛ**, *pri*, *prâ*, [*pûr*] to fill.

दे **dé** to have pity.

मे **mé** to change.

वे **vé** to weave.

खै **kæ** [*khan*] to dig; to be firm; to strike [*han*].

दै **dæ** to purify.

ध्यै **dhyæ** to contemplate.

ध्रै **dhræ** to be satiated.

ज्यो **jyó** (*jyav*) to arrange; to offer as a vow.
