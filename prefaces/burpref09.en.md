# SANSKRIT SUFFIXES.

Simple suffixes may combine with one another and give rise to compound suffixes indicating more or less numerous derivations within the same word.

The simplest suffixes consist of a short vowel, which the grammatical flexions modify in various ways.

The other simple suffixes, formed of a consonant and a vowel, are distinguished from one another by the place the consonant occupies relative to the vowel. There are thus in all three classes of suffixes, the number of which, for the last two, could still be reduced by analysis.
