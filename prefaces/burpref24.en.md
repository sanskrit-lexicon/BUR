# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 777]

**कल्** *kal*

**कुन्द्र्** *kundr, gundr* to deceive, to lie.

**कुर** *kur* to resound; *ǵur* to resound terribly; *kall* to make a vague sound, cf. *swan, swṛ*.

**क्मर्** *kmar* to be bent.

**चित्र्** *ćitr* to variegate (*ći*, sfx. *tra*).

**चूर्** *ćūr* to steal (*ćar?*); *tsar ćal* to deceive.

**छिद्र्** *ćhidr (ćhid)* to pierce.

**तीर्** *tīr (tṝ)* to pass an act.

**तूर्** *tūr twar* to hurry (*ćar?*).

**पार्** *pār pūr* to fill (*pṝ*).

**पुर्** *pur* to walk devant.

**मिश्र्** *miçr misr* to mix.

**मुर्** *mur* to wrap, to clothe.

**मूत्र्** *mūtr* to urinate (*mih*, sfx. *tra*).

**वीर्** *vīr* to be strong, to be brave.

**शार्** *çār sār* to be weak.

**सुर्** *sur, swar* to shine (to be master), *ćūr* to burn cf. *jwal [kal]* (to wound, to kill).

**अल्** *al* to adorn (to suffice).

**कल्** *kal* to go [to count, to sound, to take]. || 1° *gal* to fall (to flow; to eat cf. *gṝ*); || *xal khal* to waver, to bind, *kul* to bind, to count (to go), *kīl* to bind (to sew); *kēl, khēl kwēl xwēl* to go (to waver); || *ćal ćēl* to go (to waver, to play); *jwal* to waver (to blaze); *ćīl* to clothe; || *lil lill il* to go (to throw, to sleep); || *pal pall pēl* to go (to waver); || *val vall* to go toward (to contract, to cover oneself), *vil* to cover; *vēl vēll vēhl çēl sēl* to go (to waver, to frolic); *çal sal, çwal swal çwall* to go (to waver, to cover);

3° *ćay* to go; *ćāy* to worship; || *lay* to go (to go out, cf. *lī*; to protect, cf. *trā*); *lāy* to extend cf. *lan*, (to protect); *day* to go toward (to have pity, to love; to wound); *nay* to protect; || *pay may* to go; *xmāy* to tremble, to oscillate; || *ray vay say hay hary* to go.

---

**लल्** *lal*

**किल्** *kil* to be white, froid [to play, cf. *ćal, kal*]; *jal* to be cold (stupid).

**कूल्** *kūl* to prevent; *pīl* to prevent (to be stupefied).

**क्मील्** *xmīl çmīl smīl mīl* to blink.

**चिल्** *ćill ćull* to have leisure, to play, cf. *kil*.

**चुल्** *ćul tul* to remove, to submerge; *dul* to lift into the air; *tūl* to weigh on a scale.

**जुल्** *jul* to crush.

**टल्** *ṭal ṭwal* to be mixed.

**तल्** *tal* to found.

**तिल्** *til* to anoint.

**दल्** *dal* to split.

**नल्** *nal* to have a smell; to bind; to vex; to speak, to shine.

**निल्** *nil* to be inextricable.

**नील्** *nīl* to be blue.

**पिल्** *pil vil* to hurl, to project.

**पुल्** *pul* to be high, great.

**पूल्** *pūl, çūl [pūṇ puṇ]* to amass.

**फल्** *phal* to bear fruit.

**बिल्** *bil bhil [bhid]* to split.

**बुल्** *bul* to dive, to submerge.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
