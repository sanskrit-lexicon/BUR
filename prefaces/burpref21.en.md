# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 774]

**कर्ण्** *karṇ*

**शुङ्** *çuṅ* to limp.

**शुण्ठ्** *çunṭh* to dry up. Cf. *uṣ*.

**हड्** *haḍ heḍ eḍ* to hate.

**अड्ड्** *aḍḍ* to judge, decide.

**ईड्** *īḍ īl īl çāḍ çāl* to praise.

**कड्** *kaḍ kaṇḍ kuḍ kūḍ kṛḍ kēḷ kēḍ* to eat.

**कड्ड्** *kaḍḍ* to be bristling.

**बाड्** *bāḍ vāḍ bṛḍ druḍ kruḍ* to plunge, bathe.

**द्विड्** *dwiḍ dwid* to grind the teeth.

**गड्** *gaḍ* to flow, drip.

**जुड्** *juḍ (jun)* to bind; to go. Cf. *yu*.

**तुड्ड्** *tuḍḍ tūḍ, toḍ, roḍ raḍ raṭ, hiṇḍ heḍ hēḷ hōḍ* to disdain.

**भुण्ड्** *bhuṇḍ huṇḍ* to support, carry off.

**मुण्ड्** *muṇḍ puṇḍ* to shave, shear.

**लड्** *laḍ (lal) lanḍ luḍ (lul)* to agitate (the tongue), speak.

**व्रीड्** *vrīḍ* to be ashamed, cf. *hri*.

**हुड्** *huḍ hōḍ haḍ hruḍ hraḍ* to go.

**हेड्** *heḍ heṭ* to be reborn.

**ढुण्ड्** *ḍhuṇḍ* to search.

**ऋण्** *ṛṇ* to go (*ṛ*).

---

**श्रोण्** *çroṇ*

**कूण्** *kūṇ ćūṇ tūṇ* to contract.

**चण्** *xaṇ xiṇ ćaṇ* to kill; || *ćuṇ* to split; || cf. *xi* and *han*.

**गण्** *gaṇ* to count (cf. *kal*).

**गुण्** *guṇ kuṇ* to advise.

**घिण्** *ǵiṇ ǵuṇ ǵṛṇ* to take (*hṛ*).

**घुर्ण्** *ǵurṇ ǵūrṇ* to wander (roll).

**घृण्** *ǵṛṇ* to shine.

**चूर्ण्** *ćūrṇ* to grind.

**तुण्** *tuṇ druṇ* to be or go in a curve.

**तूण्** *tūṇ pṛṇ* to fill. (*pṝ*).

**तृण्** *tṛṇ vṛṇ* to eat.

**पण्** *paṇ baṇ* to stake (buy, sell); *baḷ* to buy, hire.

**पुण्** *puṇ* to act honestly.

**फण्** *phaṇ* to walk about, go (*path*).

**पूण्** *pūṇ* cf. *pūl*.

**भ्रूण्** *bhrūṇ* to hope, fear (*bhī, bhiru?*).

**मुण्** *muṇ* to promise.

**मृण्** *mṛṇ* to kill (*mṛ*).

**वर्ण्** *varṇ* to colour (*vṛ*); *parṇ* to be green.

**वेण्** *veṇ (vén)* to go (*vi, i*) [know, desire, praise, etc.

**व्रण्** *vraṇ* to wound.
