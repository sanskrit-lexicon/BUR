# SANSKRIT ROOTS

[page 779]

**अस्** *as*

**तृष्** *tṛṣ* to be thirsty.

**तक्ष्** *tṛx stṛx nax nēṣ bhēṣ prēṣ hrēṣ jēṣ* to go, to move, to go toward.

**त्वक्ष्** *twax tax* to split, to carpenter.

**दुष्** *duṣ* to sin.

**द्विष्** *dwiṣ* to hate.

**ध्विष्** *dhwiṣ* to resound.

**धृष्** *dhṛṣ* to face.

**पक्ष्** *pax* to take.

**पुष्** *puṣ pūṣ* to nourish.

**मुष्** *muṣ mūṣ* to steal.

**पृष्** *pṛṣ parṣ pruṣ pluṣ vṛṣ varṣ viṣ mṛṣ miṣ niṣ jiṣ ux* to water, to spread, to rain.

**मृष्** *mṛṣ miṣ* to bear, to endure.

**मृक्ष्** *mṛx mrax max* to anoint, to heap up, to split.

**यक्ष्** *yax* desid. of *yaj*.

**रक्ष्** *rax* to preserve (desid. of *rah*).

**रुक्ष्** *rūx* to be bristling (desid. of *ruh*).

**वक्ष्** *vax* (desid. of *vah*) *ḍax* to become strong.

**विष्** *viṣ* to disjoin.

**शिष्** *çiṣ* to remain over.

**सूष्** *sūṣ çūṣ* to procreate. Cf. *sū*.

**श्लिष्** *çliṣ kuç kus* to embrace, to understand.

**हृष्** *hṛṣ* to rejoice.

**अस्** *as* être.

**अस्** *as* to hurl.

**अस्** *as aṣ kaç kas nas* to go; *çaç* to go by leaps.

---

**वास्** *vās*

**अंस्** *ańs ańç* to share.

**आस्** *ās* to sit.

**कास्** *kās kāç kāńs knańs knas kuńs kuńç* to shine, to speak [*kās* to cough]; *kruç* to cry out; || *kuts jarts bharts bhas* to blame; || *vās vāç* va[…]ir; || *bhās bhas* to shine; *bhāṣ* to speak, *bhaṣ* to bark; || *bhrās bhrāç bhlās bhlūç bhṛṅç raṅç pis trańs*, to shine, to speak.

**ग्रस्** *gras glas ǵas, jax [jaǵas] ćāx, bhax bhrax bhlax bhṛx* to eat; *bhix* to beg.

**च्युस्** *ćyus* to leave, to tolerate.

**जंस्** *jańs* to protect.

**त्रस्** *tras* to tremble with fear.

**त्रस्** *tras* to hold.

**दास्** *dās* cf. *dā*.

**निंस्** *nińs nix* to kiss.

**पुंस्** *puńs pīṣ* to grind.

**प्रस्** *pras* to spread out, to procreate (*pra, as*).

**प्लुस्** *plus, çliṣ çriṣ*, to share.

**बिस्** *bis bus, mis* to go, to hurl.

**भ्यस्** *bhyas (bhī)* to be afraid.

**मस्** *mas* to measure.

**यस्** *yas yēṣ pēṣ* to strive, cf. *yat*.

**रस्** *ras rās rāç* to resound, to howl; *rēṣ* to howl; *hrēṣ hēṣ* to neigh; *hras hlas kaç ǵuṣ tus nūs maç miç* to resound, to cry out; cf. *ru* et *nad*.

**लस्** *las* to embrace; || to jump.

**विस्** *vis viṣ* to enter, to penetrate; *vas* to dwell, to clothe oneself.

**वस्** *vas* to be straight, fixed.

**वास्** *vās* to colour, to make fragrant.
